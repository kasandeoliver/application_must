Mbarara University of Science and TechnologyOnline Application Portal

Your journey to MUST starts here.

  1. Create your account and receive a temporary password.
  2. Log in and apply for our undergraduate programmes.
  3. Pay the non-refundable application fee at any bank accepting URA payments.
  4. Completed applications must be accompanied by certified copies of academic documents.
Entry requirements
  • Direct entry: five UCE passes at one sitting and two principal passes at UACE.
  • Diploma entry: a diploma with at least a Credit from an accredited institution.
  • Each programme may have extra subject requirements.
  • Foreign qualifications must be equated first: UNEB (O and A-Level) or NCHE (certificates, diplomas, degrees).
  • Non-English applicants may need proof of English proficiency.

Documents and integrity

Send certified copies to admissions@must.ac.ug or deliver them to the Academic Registrar’s office. Impersonation, falsified documents or false or incomplete information lead to automatic cancellation of admission.
